# SME Interview Kit — Wayfinder T2

**Goal:** 10–15 conversations with F&B owners (cafes, casual dining 3–10 outlets, bakeries — Klang Valley / Cyberjaya area). You are testing three hypotheses, not selling:
- **H1 (pain):** owners feel real pain around customer retention / loyalty admin today.
- **H2 (self-serve):** they would set it up themselves if it takes ~15 minutes — they don't secretly want a salesperson.
- **H3 (price):** RM299–499/month clears their value bar.

**Rules of the game:** talk to owners/decision-makers, not staff. Ask about the past, not the hypothetical future ("what do you do today" beats "would you use"). Show the prototype only in Part 4 — never first. Take notes verbatim; the exact words matter for copy later.

---

## Part 1 — Their world today (10 min)

1. Walk me through how you keep customers coming back today. (Anything — stamp cards, WhatsApp group, Instagram, nothing.)
2. Have you ever tried a loyalty app, stamp card, or membership system? What happened to it?
3. What do you currently pay for, software-wise, to run the shop? (POS, delivery platforms, marketing tools.) Roughly what per month?
4. Who set those tools up — you, staff, or the vendor's people? How did that go?
5. When a regular customer stops coming, do you notice? Do you do anything?

*Listening for: real retention pain (H1), current software spend as a price anchor (H3), and setup horror stories or "the vendor did it all" (H2 signal in either direction).*

## Part 2 — The buying moment (5 min)

6. If you were going to add a membership/points program this year, what would push you to actually do it?
7. What killed it last time you considered something like this? (Price? Time? Staff training? Didn't believe it works?)
8. Have you heard of / been pitched Pixalink, Advocado, Simple Loyalty, or similar? What was your impression?

## Part 3 — Price test (5 min) — before showing anything

Describe it in one sentence only: *"A membership system your customers use through a QR code and their phone — no app to download — with points, tiers, and rewards, under your own brand. You set it up yourself online in about 15 minutes, no sales call."*

9. At what monthly price would that feel like an obvious yes? (open answer)
10. At what monthly price would it feel expensive but still worth considering?
11. At what monthly price would you not even look at it? (Van Westendorp-lite — record all three numbers.)
12. Would you rather pay RM399/month flat, or RM299 + small per-message costs billed by WhatsApp directly? (tests billing-model tolerance)

## Part 4 — Prototype reaction (10 min)

Open `prototype-onboarding-wizard.html`, Variant A. Hand them the device. Say nothing except: *"Imagine you're setting this up for your shop. Go."* Start a timer.

13. (observe) Where do they hesitate? What do they tap that isn't tappable? Do they read or skip the hints?
14. Record their time to reach the QR step.
15. After: "Would you have finished this on your own at the shop on a normal Tuesday?"
16. "This is the setup — the customer side would look like a polished, HeyTea-style card with your logo and colours, and you'd pick from several designs. Does choosing a design yourself appeal, or is that a chore?"
17. Kill question: "What would stop you from paying for this next month?"

## Part 5 — Close (2 min)

18. "If I launch a pilot at RM___/month" (say their Q10 number) "for the first 10 shops, would you want to be one?" — **count only concrete yeses with contact details as validation.** Politeness is not data.

## One-page pitch sheet (read this aloud or hand over — Part 3 wording, expanded)

**[Working name] — your own membership program, live in 15 minutes.**

Your regulars scan a QR at the counter. Their phone opens your membership card — your logo, your colours, no app to download. They collect points every visit, unlock rewards you choose, and get their card back any time through WhatsApp.

You set it all up yourself, online, in about 15 minutes: name your tier, pick your first reward, choose a card design you like, print the QR. Transparent monthly price on the website. No sales call, no agent, no contract. Change anything any time.

Built in Malaysia, compliant with the new PDPA rules — your customer data stays yours, handled properly, so you don't have to think about it.
